The problem

Running a detailing business out of a phone.

Spot On had the demand. What it didn't have was a system. Every booking, every quote, every payment lived in text threads and DMs — which meant the business only moved as fast as its owner could reply.

  • Bookings taken through Instagram DMs and texts, with no formal confirmation or close
  • Response times stretching up to two days — leads going cold while waiting
  • Quotes sent with no tracking: no way to know who opened, who booked, who walked
  • No way to take a deposit or payment before the job
  • No way to collect tips or structured feedback
  • Every corporate session coordinated by hand, slot by slot
What we built

A booking and payment engine, end to end.

A system where a customer books, pays and schedules on their own — no back-and-forth, no manual step. The owner stopped being the bottleneck.

Self-service booking
Customers pick a service, vehicle size, add-ons, date and time, and pay by card — all in one guided flow. The price is calculated live, and the appointment is scheduled automatically.
Payments that just work
Cards are charged the moment a booking is confirmed. If a charge goes through but the slot can't be scheduled, the system refunds automatically — no customer ever pays without an appointment.
Recurring memberships
Customers can subscribe to a maintenance plan instead of booking one-off, turning one-time jobs into predictable recurring revenue.
Tips & reviews, automated
After each service, the system asks for a tip and a review through its own link — capturing revenue and reputation that were slipping through before.
Automatic follow-ups
Confirmations, reminders and review requests all send themselves on schedule. Nobody has to remember to chase anyone.
Owner dashboard
A private, secure panel to see every booking, manage cancellations, charge extras, run promos and pull reports — the whole operation in one place.
Step-by-step booking: service, vehicle, add-ons, date and payment — with the price calculated live.
Customers pick from real available slots. No back-and-forth, no phone tag.
The differentiator

A recurring corporate fleet contract, running on autopilot.

The biggest win wasn't the website — it was landing a recurring contract with a corporate legal firm and building the entire program to run itself. Scheduled sessions, fixed program pricing, and a waitlist that manages its own slots.

Scheduled sessions with limited slots
Each session runs on a fixed capacity (10 confirmed spots plus 3 on standby), booked against a specific date at a set program rate.
A waitlist that promotes itself
When a spot frees up, the system sends a payment link and promotes the next person to a confirmed slot on its own. Unpaid holds expire automatically. No manual slot-shuffling.
Session & access reminders
Coordinators get automatic reminders for sessions and key/access handoffs — coordination that used to happen by hand.
Executive reporting
The corporate client gets clean, branded reports in Spot On's own voice — professional enough for an institutional account.
A dedicated landing for the corporate program — employees book on-site office detailing in a fixed biweekly window.
